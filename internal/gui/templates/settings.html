Preferences

Renders all text in the GUI in lowercase. Purely cosmetic — your data is not changed.

Controls which day starts the week on the calendar and snapshot views.

Controls how bottom tray inventory is counted.
Flood tables — your lit environment has automated watering (flood tables, drip lines, etc.), so bottom trays are returned to inventory when trays move to light.
Tray pairs — every grow tray sits in a bottom tray for the entire cycle. Both are returned together at harvest.

Changing units only swaps the labels — it does NOT convert any numbers. You will need to update your crop values (seed weight, yield, medium, etc.) manually after switching.

Switches the colour scheme between dark and light mode.

Enables the refined visual style — subtle shadows, gradients, smoother transitions, and a modernised swim-lane calendar. Turn it off to go back to the original clean look.

When the display refreshes at midnight, automatically pull the latest data from Google Sheets first — so the new day starts with everything up to date. Only works if Google Sheets is linked on the Sync page.
